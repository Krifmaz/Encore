## Encore 0.40.0

**The apps use your home network on their own.** Set the server's address at home once, and the desktop, phone
and TV apps switch to it whenever they're on your home network, and back to the public address when they leave.

### Local network address
- **Settings → General → Local network address** (or `ENCORE_LOCAL_URL`), next to *Public URL*: the address Encore
  answers at on your home network, for example `http://10.0.1.7:8080`.
- **Desktop, Android and Android TV apps** ask the server for both addresses after you sign in, try the local one
  when the network changes (and every minute on the desktop), and use it while it answers. Songs, covers and pages
  then come straight over your home network instead of through the internet and your reverse proxy.
- **Away from home** a call that can't reach the local address goes to the public one at once, so nothing stops.
  An app you signed in to at the home address finds the public one by itself (it remembers both).
- **Safe switching**: an app only moves to an address after it answered as *this* server (a new `server_id` in
  `/api/auth/info`), so your session never goes to another device that happens to have the same address.
- **Which one is in use**: the desktop app's Settings → Account (*Connected through: Local network (…)*), and the
  phone's Settings → Account row (*(local network)* / *(public address)*). The desktop app also says so in a toast
  when it switches.
- **Website**: a browser keeps the address it was opened at, since its sign-in belongs to that address. Settings →
  Account says which address the browser uses and links the local one.
- For apps and scripts: `GET /api/auth/addresses` (signed in) answers `{public, local, server_id}`; the home
  network's address is not shown to anyone signed out.

### Before you update
- Nothing to do. Enter the Local network address in Settings → General to turn it on.
