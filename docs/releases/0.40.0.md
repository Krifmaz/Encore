## Encore 0.40.0

**Recommendations made on your own server.** Smart shuffle, mixes made for you, genre shuffles, a dislike button and
"Start radio" – like YouTube Music or Spotify, but worked out by Encore from your library and your listening. No
outside service is asked anything.

### What it learns from
- **Plays** (they fade over a few months, so your taste can move), **Liked Songs** (a strong yes), the songs on
  **your own playlists**, **skips** (Next before a song's play counted: a mild "not now"; a song skipped again and
  again sits out for a few weeks) and **dislikes** (a hard no).
- Every one of those counts for the song itself, its artists, its **genres** and its **era**. Genres come from the
  songs' genre tags; an untagged song takes its artist's usual genres.
- **Co-listening:** artists played in the same listening sessions on this server count as close, so a song by an
  artist you never played can still come up when it sits next to what you like.

### Smart shuffle
- Settings → Playback → **Smart shuffle** (on by default): shuffles and the music after the queue lean to what you
  play and like. Favourites come up more often, songs you just heard wait a few hours, the same artist never plays
  twice in a row, and anything you disliked never plays. Off, a shuffle is plain random as before.
- With it on, shuffling a playlist or an album puts it in a taste-weighted order (disliked songs left out).

### Dislike
- A **thumbs-down** next to the heart in the player (web bar and full player, Android phone and TV). A disliked song
  leaves Liked Songs and Up next, the player moves on, and no shuffle, mix or radio plays it again. Tap it again to
  take it back; a like takes it back too.
- ⋯ → **Don't play <artist>** keeps an artist out of shuffles and mixes. The plain shuffle leaves disliked songs and
  artists out as well.
- On the web the bar gets the button once; *Customize the bar…* can hide it.

### For you: mixes from your library
- **Your mix**, **Fresh finds** (songs you haven't played yet that fit your taste), **Rediscover** (favourites you
  haven't heard in a month), a mix for each of your favourite genres and a **radio** for each of your favourite
  artists. Each one is made again when you play it.
- **Your genres**: one tap plays a genre's mix. **Genre shuffle…** (on For you and in the player's ⋯ menu on the
  web) shuffles one or several genres together, and the music after the queue keeps to them.
- The ListenBrainz and Last.fm recommendations stay below them.

### Start radio
- ⋯ → **Start radio** on the song playing: that song, then songs like it from the library – its artist and the ones
  heard with it, the same genres, a close era – leaning to your taste.

### For apps and scripts
- `POST /api/taste/shuffle` `{limit, exclude, genre | genres | ids}`, `GET /api/taste/mixes`, `POST /api/taste/mix`
  `{id, limit, exclude}`, `GET /api/taste/like-this/{id}`, `GET /api/taste/genres`, `GET /api/taste/profile`,
  `POST /api/taste/skip` `{track_id, seconds}`, `GET /api/taste/dislikes`, `PUT`/`DELETE
  /api/taste/dislikes/tracks/{id}` and `/api/taste/dislikes/artists/{name}`.

### Before you update
- Nothing to do. The database gains two tables (dislikes, skips); a backup is made before the change as always.
