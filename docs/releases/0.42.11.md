## Encore 0.42.11

**Singles play as songs instead of opening an album page.** A release that has one song in your library (a
single like ">one" by VALORANT & ericdoa) no longer opens an album page with one row on it. Tapping it plays the
song and opens the full player, the same as tapping a song.

- **Library, Home and search:** a single's tile says *Song · artist · year*. A tap plays it; the play button
  does the same.
- **Artist pages:** singles you already have play on a tap, both in *In your library* and in the discography
  (MusicBrainz and *Not in MusicBrainz* releases). Singles you don't have yet still open their release page, where
  you can request them.
- Releases with two songs or more keep their album page.

### Details
- The album page is still there for the tools only it has (*Tag this album*, *Change type…*, *Pick download*,
  *Delete album*). Get to it from the song's ⋯ → *Go to album*.
- Nothing changes on the server's side: singles are still releases, with the same tags, files and album id.
  Discography answers now carry `library_tracks` (how many songs the library's copy has) next to
  `library_album_id`.
- Works in the web app, the desktop app and the Android app (on a TV the TV player opens). The phone and desktop
  apps get it with the next full release.
