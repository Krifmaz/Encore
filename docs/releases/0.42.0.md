## Encore 0.42.0

**Lyrics that run early or late can be fixed from the player.** Nudge the time-synced lines until they match the
song, or ask for another timing, right where you read them. The nudge is saved on the server, so the TV and a phone
controlling it show the same line.

### Timing nudge
- Under time-synced lyrics (Android phone, web, desktop): **Earlier** and **Later** move every line a quarter second,
  and the value between them (*+0.75 s*, *−1.5 s*) puts the lines back *In sync* when tapped.
- One nudge per song, for every device and listener. It belongs to the lyrics it was made on: when a report brings
  other lyrics, they start in sync again.
- A `.lrc` file's own `[offset:]` tag is honoured too (the file is left as it is), so lyrics you timed in another
  player keep their offset in Encore.
- The TV and Android Auto follow the nudge; they have no buttons of their own.

### Other timing
- **Other timing** next to *Report lyrics* opens the report with *Lyrics out of sync* ticked: this timing goes and
  another time-synced version is looked up at once (the same words without timing if there is none). *Report
  lyrics* still opens with *Wrong lyrics* ticked, for words that belong to another song.

### Remote control
- A phone controlling another device (the TV, the web app) now follows that device's position ten times a second for
  the lyrics, instead of every half second, so a line lights up when it is sung there. The web app moves a linked
  device's position on four times a second instead of once.

### Remixes get their own lyrics
- The LRCLIB search no longer gives a remix, live take, acoustic or other version the original's lyrics (or the
  original a remix's): the words in brackets or after " - " (*Remix*, *Live*, *Acoustic*, *Edit*, *Version*…) have to
  match. A song whose lyrics were wrong for this reason: *Report lyrics → Wrong lyrics* finds the right ones.

### For apps and scripts
- `GET /api/library/tracks/{id}/lyrics` answers `shift_ms` (how much later the time-synced lines are shown; negative:
  earlier). `PUT /api/library/tracks/{id}/lyrics/shift` `{shift_ms}` saves it (±30 s; 0 takes it away).

### Before you update
- Nothing to do. The database gains one table (lyrics_shift; schema 41); a backup is made before the change as always.
