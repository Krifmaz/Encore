## Encore 0.26.1

- **"This computer (Cadenza)" is gone from Your devices.** The Cadenza engine built into the desktop app used to sign in as a device of its own the moment the app opened, whether you used it or not. It is no longer listed anywhere (web or phone), nothing links to it on its own, and a saved link to it is forgotten so music plays on the computer itself again. A standalone Cadenza on your network still shows up. In 0.27 the engine comes back as a switch under Settings → Playback, not as a device.
- Everything from 0.26.0 is unchanged; the desktop and Android apps are the 0.26.0 builds.
