## Encore 0.25.0

### Playback fixes
- **Songs no longer skip halfway or stop by themselves on the website** (phones on mobile data especially). When a stream breaks off, Encore picks the song back up at the same second instead of jumping to the next one, and retries after network errors and when the connection comes back.
- **Auto quality never drops to 32 kbps** any more: 96 kbps is the floor (64 only on a very poor link), and it climbs back up faster.
- **Download missing** on a playlist no longer stops at a song without an artist or title; it skips that one and requests the rest.

### Search
- **"Artist + song" just works**: "travis scott 90210" puts Travis Scott – *90210* (from *Rodeo*) first. Encore asks MusicBrainz, Deezer and Apple together and pushes jokes, karaoke and uploader junk down.
- **Top result** first, then songs (with their album), albums and artists; your library first, with Play.
- **Typos and accents forgiven** in library search ("trvis scot 90210").

### Library
- **Sort ▾ on every tab** (Albums, Artists, Songs) and the **side bar follows the sort**: years, months, lengths or letters – also for libraries added all at once.
- **Faster covers**: colour placeholders while art loads, a faster background pass (recent and played albums first), look-ahead loading on the phone.
- **Shuffle, Repeat and Lyrics** buttons in the full player (phone and web), in sync with the car and other devices.
- **Tap an artist or album name** on any song to open its page.

### Artists, albums and requests
- **Play whatever you own** from artist and album pages, even when only some songs of an album are yours.
- **Request discography** includes albums only Deezer or Apple know.
- **Live labels**: Requested → Searching → Downloading → In library (or Not found / Failed) update as they happen.
- **Release types** (Settings → Apps & sources → Download sources), Lidarr-style: which kinds to request (Album, EP, Single…) and which extras to skip (Live, Remix, Instrumental, Interview, Compilation, Soundtrack, Acoustic, Sped up/Slowed, Karaoke, Covers, DJ-mix, Mixtape, Demo…). Request discography lets you change them for one artist. Song downloads skip live/remix/instrumental versions unless you asked for one.
- **Single songs from torrents and Usenet**: your indexers are searched for the song, and an album release can be grabbed with only that song's file downloaded.

### Bad files
- **Report a problem** on any song or album (wrong song, wrong version, bad quality, too quiet, fake lossless…): that exact copy is blocked, the file goes to the trash for 7 days, and Encore fetches another copy – plays, likes and playlists stay.
- **Downloads are checked** for fake lossless (cut-off highs), very quiet or clipped audio and the wrong version before they enter the library; an optional library scan finds old ones.

### YouTube sign-in that stays signed in
- **Sign in to YouTube inside Encore** (Settings → YouTube; needs the *Browser for YouTube sign-in* Download tool): Encore keeps the sign-in and renews the cookies on its own.
- **Browser extension** (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox – attached to this release) that sends fresh cookies to Encore.
- **Pasted cookies are kept alive** with Google's own refresh call.

### Giving back
- Settings → Contribute: send fingerprints of verified songs to **AcoustID**, an **Add to MusicBrainz** button that opens MusicBrainz's form already filled in for albums it's missing, and ISRC submissions after connecting your MusicBrainz account.

### More
- **Amazon Music** public playlist and album links import (private library: not yet).
- **Translations**: official translated / romanized titles from MusicBrainz, then a local AI model (Download tool *Translator*, small or best). Per user: Settings → Language. Corrections stick.
- **Car**: the Lyrics button turns the cover into a live lyrics card; **Encore Lyrics** is a second car app with scrolling lyrics that leaves Google Maps navigating.
- **Desktop app**: taskbar buttons and progress (Windows), dock menu (macOS), mini player, global hotkeys, start with the computer, offline songs through Cadenza, and auto-update.
- The database moves to schema 25 (backed up first).
