## Encore 0.26.4

- **Bug reports in the app.** A new *Bug reports* entry sits in the side bar under the storage box. Anyone signed in writes a report – kind Bug, Not a bug or How do I…, a title, what happened, and up to 6 screenshots or text files (8 MB each). The page you came from, the app version and your browser go along by themselves, so you do not have to type them.
- **The admin reviews and answers in the app.** The list filters by status (New · Looking into it · Fixed · Won't fix · Answered) and kind, searches, and shows everyone's or only your own reports. Open one to set its status, read the thread and reply with text and images; the reporter sees the reply in the app, and by email when email is set up. A badge on the side bar entry counts what is new for you.
- **Export for the developer.** *Export* on the list (with the current filters) or *Export this report* on one report downloads a zip with the reports as Markdown and JSON plus the images.
- Server image plus the web UI; the desktop app shows the same pages. The phone app's screen follows later. Reload the web app once after updating.
