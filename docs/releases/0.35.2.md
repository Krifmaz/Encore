## Encore 0.35.2

**Imports no longer wait for ages, and group listen stops skipping.**

### Imports
After an update or restart, a few hundred waiting requests all asked MusicBrainz which album their song is on at
the same time, and MusicBrainz takes one question a second. Every import waited behind them, so one song could
take 10–20 minutes to import.
- Imports and the pages you open now go first in MusicBrainz's line; background work waits behind them. A lookup
  keeps one place in the line for all its questions.
- An import says what it is doing: "Importing – waiting for MusicBrainz (about 2 minutes, 120 lookups ahead)",
  "– checking the audio", "– moving into the library".
- "Looking up which album this song is on…" ends: each try waits at most 5 minutes, the album Deezer names for the
  song is used when MusicBrainz can't answer (never a live album, best-of or compilation), and after 3 busy tries
  the song is downloaded on its own. What MusicBrainz answered is kept for a day.
- A restart keeps what was already looked up, and an import reuses the album lookup's answer.
- Auto-download asks for a failed song again after 15 minutes, then 30, 60 … at most once a day, instead of every
  15 minutes. "Download missing" pressed by you still asks at once.
- A Deezer track that isn't available in your country now says so plainly.
- Pick on a request that is importing says "It is being imported now" instead of pointing at a button that isn't there.

### Group listen
- **No more small skips.** Players that drift apart catch up by playing up to 2 % faster or slower, which you can't
  hear; a jump happens only past 1.5 seconds. Quality stays the same for a whole song in a group, the timing is
  measured more carefully, and a hiccup on one phone no longer makes everyone jump back.
- **Shuffle works in a group**: the server shuffles once, so everyone hears the same order.
- **Clear buttons**: Clear in the player's Up next (with Undo), Clear and Shuffle in the group's Up next.
- While a group plays, the loudness measuring and the Translator model pause.

### Requests
- **Clear waiting** on the Requests page cancels every request still in line or waiting. It asks first with the
  count; downloads and imports already running, and requests holding downloaded files, are left alone.
