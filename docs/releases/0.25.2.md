## Encore 0.25.2

- **Guided YouTube sign-in**: Settings → YouTube → Sign in to YouTube now asks for your email, password and (if Google wants one) your authenticator code in Encore's own boxes – your phone keyboard works, and Encore fills Google's page for you. A security-key or passkey prompt is skipped automatically ("Try another way" → authenticator app); text-message and backup codes work too. Your password isn't stored.
- **Live view fixed** (the fallback for unusual Google screens): taps reach the page, and typing works on phone keyboards.
