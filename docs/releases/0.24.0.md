## Encore 0.24.0

### Pick up where you left off
- **Every app remembers its spot**: web, desktop and phone reopen paused on the song and position where you stopped (with the queue). Setting: *Settings → Playback → Resume where I left off when the app opens*.
- **Continue on another device**: open any app and it offers "Continue *song* from 2:31 · was playing on *device*" with **Play here**, which moves the music over and pauses the other device.

### Search and artists
- **Best match first**: exact names, then names that start with what you typed, then words, then anywhere. "ye" lists Ye first.
- **Artists found through their albums, songs and other names**: "808" finds Ye through *808s & Heartbreak*; "kanye" finds Ye (names from MusicBrainz, looked up in the background).
- **One artist per artist**: "The Black Eyed Peas" and "Black Eyed Peas" are one. Collaborations like "Kanye West & XXXTENTACION" show under each artist – only when every part is an artist of its own, so bands like "Simon & Garfunkel" stay whole.
- **Duplicate songs are hidden** in search results (the best copy is kept).
- **Phone artist page**: Play and Shuffle.

### Sharing
- **Share albums and artists** from every app. The link opens a page with the cover and a playable song list.
- **Link preview setting** (*Settings → General → Shared link preview*): *Small card*, *Big picture* or *Playable card* (Discord/X show a play button and seek bar).

### Downloads
- **More torrent clients**: qBittorrent, Transmission, Deluge, rTorrent / ruTorrent, or a blackhole (watch) folder.
- **More Usenet clients**: SABnzbd, NZBGet, or a blackhole folder. Settings show only the chosen client's fields; *Test connection* works for each. Existing qBittorrent/SABnzbd settings carry over.

### Phone app
- **Joins your devices**: shows up in your other apps, can be controlled from them, and has a **Devices** button: *Play here*, *Send my music there*, *Link as remote*. Remembers the link. *Settings → This device → Device name*.
- **Side scroll bar** on Albums, Artists, Songs and artist pages: A–Z, years, months or lengths depending on the sort; drag for a big label and a jump. (Also on the web library.)
- **Faster covers**: covers are cached for good and only fetched again when the art changes; small WebP covers are made ahead of time after each scan; the phone keeps a 250 MB cover cache.
- **Song transitions**: *Off*, *Crossfade*, *Overlap* (next song at full volume, the old one fades under it) and *Smart overlap* (silence at the ends trimmed first). Crossfade now actually works on the phone, and on the web it no longer stays silent when the window is hidden or the screen is off.
- **Android Auto**: a Songs section, A–Z folders (fewer "scrolling paused" stops), the on-screen search button, and **Like**, **Shuffle**, **Repeat** and **Lyrics** buttons – Lyrics shows the live lyric line on the car screen.
- **Crashes are caught**: every crash is saved and shown under *Diagnostics → App crashes*, and if the app dies while playing, it picks up again at the same spot by itself.

### Also
- Long times read naturally: "4 mo 22 days 8 hr 24 min" instead of "3416 hr 24 min".
- Soulseek shows *running · scanning shares 11%* while slskd builds its first share list (a big library takes a while once).
- The database moves to schema 22 (backed up first, as always).
