## Encore 0.33.0

The first release since 0.30.0. It brings together what was written for 0.31, 0.32 and 0.33 (none of them was released on its own): group listen and the player features of 0.31, plugins in 0.32, and in 0.33 the file manager and the work since then: the car, error messages on the phone, shared playlists, *Download now*, download folders that can't be written, and translated titles without the local model. The short version is below; the full notes of 0.31 and 0.32 are linked after it, and the 0.33 part follows in full.

### Fixed since 0.30.0
- **Android Auto showed three buttons and ⋯, and two of them did nothing.** The row had six buttons (Like, a quality badge, Artist, Shuffle, Repeat, Lyrics). A car with four spots showed three of them and put the rest behind ⋯, and pressing the quality badge or Artist did nothing, because Android Auto lets a music app neither open its own pages from a button nor resize its player. The row is now **Shuffle, Repeat, Like, Lyrics**: four buttons and no ⋯. The quality is on the line under the title, the artist is *More by <artist>* at the top of Browse, and *Car buttons* in Settings changes the row (see *Android Auto* below).
- **The car's lyrics were hard to read.** The line being sung in the cover is about 2.2 times taller, with only the next line below it, and Encore Lyrics gives the lyrics the whole screen.
- **Errors on the phone showed behind sheets and dialogs** and went away after about ten seconds, so they could not be copied. Messages now show above every screen of the app, sheets, dialogs and the full player included; an error stays until you close it, shows its whole text and has *Copy*.
- **A read-only, full or missing downloads disk failed request after request** (Oct 17: yt-dlp's "Read-only file system (os error 30)" on every request). Encore now checks every folder downloads are saved in, Soulseek's and the download clients' too. While one can't be written, the requests that save there wait before they start ("Waiting for the downloads folder"), admins see one banner on every page, and the requests go on by themselves once it is fixed.
- **Liked Songs pulled from a service** went in a different order at every sync. They now keep the order the service lists them in (newest like first for Spotify, Deezer, YouTube Music, Last.fm and ListenBrainz), and songs already in Liked Songs keep their places. A playlist imported from Spotify without a sign-in no longer gets a note written into its description.
- **Sign-ins started in the phone or desktop app** (Spotify, Tidal, Last.fm, MusicBrainz) no longer need you signed in to Encore in the browser first.
- **A visitor can no longer pick their own address** with an `X-Forwarded-For` header to get around the sign-in limits: Encore reads it only on connections from a trusted proxy, and takes the address that proxy added (see *Before you update* if your proxy has a public address).
- **Smaller fixes:** the Signal path writes 22050 Hz as "22.05 kHz" (it said "22.1 kHz") on the website, the desktop app and the phone; sharing an album or an artist says "this album" or "this artist" (it said "this song"), and the website shows that message once instead of twice; the desktop app's Home tile keeps "leaderboard" inside the card.

### New since 0.30.0
- **Group listen** (0.31): several people on this server hear the same queue at the same time, each on their own device. Start it from the player's ⋯ menu or Your devices; others join with the code, the invite link or Home's *Listening together now*. The host controls playback unless *Everyone can control* is on, everyone adds songs to Up next, and when the admin and the host allow it, people without an account can listen from the link.
- **Player features** (0.31): volume normalization (Off, Track or Album, with a target level; the server measures the songs without ReplayGain tags at night), a lyrics mode in the full player, *Liked from this artist* and *In your library* on artist pages, one search over the whole library that also takes initials ("rhcp", "crazy gb"), Discover showing what your library has first, and long titles that scroll on the website and in the desktop app.
- **Plugins** (0.32): the download sources, download clients, Cadenza server processing, the Translator and the Jellyfin, Plex and Navidrome connections are plugins under Settings → Plugins, each installed, updated, switched off, tested and removed on its own, without a new Encore image. Plugins written by other people install from a zip or a URL and run in a sandbox. An upgraded server keeps everything it used.
- **Files** (0.33): a file manager for admins over the library, the staging and download folders and Trash: upload (folders too), move, rename, trash and restore, edit tags, and *Import…* a folder into the library, moved in or kept where it is.
- **Android Auto** (0.33): the four buttons and the **Car buttons** setting (Settings → Playback → In the car), which puts each of Shuffle, Repeat, Like, Lyrics, Artist and Signal path on the car screen, in the ⋯ menu or hides it, in your order, with a preview of what your car will show. The quality is on the line under the title ("DROELOE · FLAC 24/96"), *More by <artist>* starts Browse, the lyrics in the cover are as large as they fit, and **Encore Lyrics** draws the lyrics over the whole screen with the blurred cover behind them and the controls in one small strip. What Android Auto keeps for itself (the size of its player, the card beside Encore Lyrics) is described below.
- **Shared playlists** (0.33): *Share* on any playlist, Liked Songs included, makes a link that always plays the playlist as it is now, until you press *Stop sharing*. Anyone with the link can play it on a web page; people on your server can **add it to their own Playlists** (it follows your changes and stays read-only for them) or save a copy.
- **Download now** (0.33) on a waiting or queued request: it goes first in its source's line, a wait for the storage, the downloads folder or Soulseek is tried now, a Soulseek download stuck in someone's upload queue moves to a user with a free slot, and a torrent with no progress for five minutes gives way to the next source. It never runs more at once than a source allows. Admins also get *Download all now*.
- **Download folders that can't be written** (0.33): the check and the waiting described under *Fixed*, with each folder's state in Diagnostics → Download folders.
- **Translated titles without the local model** (0.33): official names from MusicBrainz and Apple, romanization built into the server (Japanese kana, Korean, Cyrillic, Greek and more), and **DeepL** with your own API key, before the local model, which is now optional and last. Settings → Language shows what each source does; admins switch them there.

### Before you update
- **The database moves from schema 26 (0.30) to 29 on the first start**, in three steps: 27 (loudness, 0.31), 28 (plugins, 0.32) and 29 (translation sources, 0.33). Before the first step Encore saves a copy of the database in the `backups` folder of `/config` (`encore-…-before-upgrade.db`, listed in Settings → Diagnostics → Backups).
- **There is no going back to 0.30 without that copy.** 0.30 does not open the upgraded database: it stops with "This database was written by a newer Encore (schema 29, this server knows 26) …" and changes nothing. To go back, stop the container, copy the before-upgrade file over `encore.db` in the config folder (and delete `encore.db-wal` and `encore.db-shm` if they are there), then start the 0.30 image. Whatever was saved after the upgrade is lost. (*Restore…* in 0.33 would upgrade the copy again.)
- **Plugins take over what you use, with no new setup.** On the first start every tool that was in use is taken over where it is in `/config` (nothing is moved) and switched on with its settings: yt-dlp always (pasted links went through it whatever *YouTube Music* said), streamrip, Soulseek, Cadenza, Prowlarr with your torrent and Usenet client, and Jellyfin, Plex or Navidrome. The activity log names what was taken over. The image no longer contains yt-dlp and streamrip: if yours ran on the image's own copies, the plugins install them into `/config/plugins/` in the background, which needs the network once; requests that need them wait meanwhile ("Waiting for the streamrip plugin to finish installing", at most an hour), and a failed install is tried again by itself. `docker exec encore yt-dlp` and `docker exec encore streamrip` no longer work.
- **The Translator is a plugin now.** A fresh install does not install it. An upgrade takes it over only when its program and its model are both in `/config/tools/translator`. Translated titles no longer need it: official names and the built-in romanization are on by default, and DeepL works once you add a key. On the first start the built-in romanization goes over the library once: romanizations the local model made for kana, Korean, Cyrillic or Greek titles are replaced by the built-in ones (titles with kanji keep the model's), and with a DeepL key the model's translations of song and album titles are replaced by DeepL's. Corrections by hand and official names stay. To keep the model's, set `TRANSLATE_ROMANIZE=false` (and `TRANSLATE_DEEPL=false`) before the first start; switched off later under Settings → Language, they stop replacing from then on.
- **Without Docker**, the yt-dlp and streamrip plugins need uv and Python 3.14 on the host (uv on Encore's PATH, python3.14 in /usr/bin or /usr/local/bin); the Docker image has both. Debian 13 and Ubuntu 24.04 ship an older Python, so there *Install* on those plugins fails until 3.14 is added. A yt-dlp already on the PATH keeps working.
- **The plugin sandbox on Docker** says *Limits only, see the compose option*: Docker's default seccomp and AppArmor profiles stop bubblewrap. Nothing needs to change for the catalog's plugins. For full isolation of plugins written by other people, add `security_opt: [seccomp=unconfined, apparmor=unconfined]` to the encore service (it is in `docker-compose.yml`, commented out; the README's *The plugin sandbox and the compose option* explains what it switches off).
- **A reverse proxy with a public address** (Cloudflare without a tunnel, a proxy on another server): set `ENCORE_TRUSTED_PROXIES` to its addresses or networks, otherwise every visitor counts as the proxy for the sign-in limits. A proxy in another container or on your LAN is trusted by default.
- **Measure loudness** (a new scheduled task) reads every song's tags once, then measures the songs without them a few hundred per run while nobody uses the app; on a large library the first runs take a few nights.
- **Download folders:** Encore's own folders (the staging folder, Soulseek's downloads folder, the watch folders) have to be writable, or the requests that save there wait. A download client's own folder mounted read-only in Encore's container is fine: the client writes it in its own container, and Diagnostics notes it.
- **Files:** the file manager sees the folders as the container sees them, so a folder you want to import and keep where it is has to be mounted first; Trash lives in `/config/trash`.
- **Only if you changed them:** the streamrip *Command template (advanced)* is no longer run through a shell; `YTDLP_AUTO_UPDATE=false` now keeps *Update plugins* from updating yt-dlp; scripts calling `/api/system/tools` should move to `/api/plugins` (the old routes answer for this release).
- **The apps:** the phone app offers the update by itself (Settings → This device → Updates), and so does the desktop app. Update them with the server: group listen, shared playlists, *Download now*, the Plugins page, Files and the car changes are in the 0.33 apps.

A key with a typo should say "the API key was refused".
- **And on your server**: the upgrade itself (the before-upgrade backup, Settings → Plugins with yt-dlp, streamrip and Soulseek installed and your settings on their cards, one request from each source), the sandbox line on your Docker host, group listen with a second account, and a shared playlist opened on a phone that is not signed in. ### The 0.33 part

#### Android Auto
Android Auto draws the music player itself: its playback bar, the box for the cover and how many buttons fit beside Previous, Play and Next. A music app can't resize any of them or open its own pages from a button. What Encore controls is which buttons it sends and in what order, the text lines, the picture in the cover box and the second car app, Encore Lyrics, where Encore draws the screen.

- **The row is Shuffle, Repeat, Like, Lyrics** by default, after Previous, Play and Next. Every button is sent for the overflow slot only, so Previous and Next keep their places and a four-spot car shows all four with no ⋯.
- **Car buttons** (Settings → Playback → In the car → *Car buttons*, on the phone): three groups, *On the car screen*, *In the ⋯ menu* and *Hidden*. Move a button with ▲ and ▼ (at the edge of a group it crosses into the next one), with the ⋮ menu (*Move to the car screen*, *Move to the ⋯ menu*, *Hide*), or hold it and drag it. *Spots in your car* (2 to 8, 4 by default) is only for the preview: it shows the icons the car will show, "In your car: Shuffle · Repeat · Like · ⋯" and "Behind ⋯: Lyrics, Artist, Signal path", and warns in amber when Like lands behind ⋯. The car gets the screen group, then the menu group, in exactly your order; hidden buttons are never sent. *Reset to default* puts the buttons back (Shuffle, Repeat, Like, Lyrics on the screen, Artist and Signal path hidden) and leaves the spot count alone, with *Undo*. The page works with TalkBack, which reads ⋯ as "the More button". Only on this phone; while the phone is connected to Android Auto, the phone's own media controls show the same buttons.
- **Artist** (hidden by default; "More by DROELOE" in the car): adds up to 25 of the artist's songs from your library right after the playing song, without interrupting it ("Adding songs by DROELOE…", then "Added 25 songs by DROELOE after this song", "… to the group's Up next" in a group listen, or "No other songs by DROELOE in your library"). A second press says they are already added.
- **Signal path** (hidden by default): the whole path on the line under the title for 10 seconds ("FLAC 24/96 → as is → Android 48 kHz", "FLAC 16/44.1 → AAC 96k (Data saver) → Android 48 kHz", "Saved FLAC 24/96 → bit-perfect to <your DAC>", with " · −3.2 dB" when normalization changes the level).
- **The quality on the line under the title**, always while a car is connected: "DROELOE · FLAC 24/96", "… · AAC 256", "… · FLAC → AAC 96" (Data saver or a slow connection), "… · FLAC → Cadenza 192 kHz". With live lyrics in the title line it reads "Gravity · DROELOE · FLAC 24/96", so a car that cuts the line short drops the quality, never the song.
- **More by <artist>** starts Browse → Home while a song is loaded, with the song's cover: *Shuffle <artist>*, *Popular songs*, *Albums* and *Liked from this artist* (rows with nothing behind them are left out). It follows the song that plays.
- **The lyrics in the cover** (*Live lyrics in the car* with *Card*): only the line being sung, bold and as large as it fits (up to about 16 % of the box's height, 7.5 % before), wrapped evenly, with the next line smaller below it, over the darkened, blurred cover. The box itself stays the size Android Auto gives it.
- **Encore Lyrics** (open it from the car's app list): the lyrics fill the free part of the screen: the line being sung large in the middle, the next one below, up to two earlier lines dimmed above, a new line sliding in; a tap on a line plays from there. The song's cover, blurred and darkened, fills the screen behind them. The controls are one strip of icons at the top: Previous, Play/Pause, Next and a person icon that adds the artist's songs as Artist does. Beside the lyrics Android Auto always keeps a card; Encore keeps it to one row with no header: the song with its cover, "artist · album" and a heart, and a tap on it opens *More by <artist>*.
  - **Its limits:** Encore Lyrics is a point-of-interest app with the map template, the largest screen such an app may draw on itself (the full-screen template is for navigation apps only). So the card beside the lyrics can't be removed and its width is Android Auto's choice, the strip holds at most four icons, and on some cars the screen may not pass taps on a line to the app.

#### Error messages on the phone
- Messages (toasts) are drawn in a window of their own above every screen of the app: sheets, dialogs, the image viewer, menus and the full player. While the keyboard is open they sit just above it.
- An error stays until you close it (X), shows its whole text (it scrolls when long) and has **Copy**, followed by "Copied to clipboard". Other messages go after four seconds. New messages go below the ones on screen instead of waiting for them; the same message again moves to the newest place instead of piling up.
- On Android 9 and older the app can't list its own windows, so a dialog opened from inside a sheet while an error shows may cover it until the next message comes or the dialog closes.

#### Sharing playlists
- **Share** on a playlist's page and on Liked Songs (website, desktop app and phone) makes a link and copies it ("Link copied – anyone with it can play this playlist"); the phone also opens the share sheet. A shared playlist shows *Shared · Anyone with the link can play these songs* with *Copy link* and *Stop sharing*, and its tile says "· shared". *Stop sharing* asks first ("The link stops working for everyone who has it. Sharing again makes a new link.").
- **The link is live**: it always shows the playlist as it is now (Liked Songs newest first), only with songs that are in the library. A song you remove or unlike stops playing from the link at once. Stopping the share, deleting the playlist, or a disabled or deleted owner ends the link: "This link doesn't work any more".
- **The page** (no account needed): a picture of four album covers (or one cover when there are fewer albums), the name, "Playlist · <owner>" or "Liked songs · <owner>", the description as plain text, the number of songs and the length, *Play*, *Shuffle* and each song to play on its own. Long playlists load as you scroll. Signed in to this server, people also get *Add to my playlists* and *Save a copy*. Chat apps preview the link as "Name · owner" with "12 songs · Shared from Encore".
- Only the owner can share a playlist. The page shows the owner's username, never the account's id, and the playlist's picture and video are marked so that shared caches don't keep them after the link ends.

#### Other people's playlists in yours
- **Add a shared playlist** on the Playlists page (website, desktop app, phone) takes a pasted link; the share page has *Add to my playlists*; on the phone an Encore playlist link shared into the app asks to add it. Adding the same link again opens what you have ("Already in your playlists"), and you can't add your own.
- **An added playlist is the owner's playlist as it is now**, shown as "Playlist · by anna" with "Changes anna makes show here. Save a copy to make your own.": *Play*, *Download for offline*, *Save a copy* (an ordinary playlist of yours) and *Remove from my playlists*. It can't be renamed or changed, *Add to playlist* doesn't offer it, merging and syncing to a media server leave it out, and Android Auto lists it with your playlists.
- **When the owner stops sharing** (or deletes it, or the account is disabled), it reads "anna stopped sharing this playlist" with *Remove*. A new link of the same playlist takes the old entry over when you add it.
- **The owner sees how many people added it** ("Anyone with the link can play these songs · added by 3 people"), never who; the activity log says "Someone added anna's playlist 'Road trip' to their playlists" (for Liked Songs: "Someone added anna's Liked Songs to their playlists").
- No database change: an added playlist is a playlist row of yours of a new kind.

#### Download now
A **Download now** button on a request that waits or is queued (Requests, Home's downloads in progress and the Downloader page on the website and the desktop app; the rows and the details sheet on the phone), for the person who asked for it and for admins. Its tooltip says what it will do; it shows only where it can do something. The rule over all of it: it never runs more at once than a source allows, never approves a request, and never tries a blocked file, user or release again.

- **Lines.** Each source with a limit now has a visible line: streamrip "1 download at a time", yt-dlp "2 downloads at a time", torrents and Usenet "2 searches at a time", Soulseek "1 search at a time", and each plugin you added one download at a time. A waiting request reads "In line for Deezer", "Next in line for Deezer", "2nd in line for Deezer". *Download now* moves it to the front, behind requests pressed earlier ("2nd in line for Deezer (1 download at a time), behind 1 request asked for earlier"), or answers "Starting now" when a slot is free. A running download is never stopped for it. The press also counts in the next line the request enters; a restart forgets presses.
- **Waits with a timer are tried now**: a storage wait checks the folder now ("The storage can be written again – downloading now", or the next try), a wait for the downloads folder probes it now, also moments after the last probe when that one found it failing ("The downloads folder can be written again – downloading now", or "The downloads folder still can't be written – …").
- **Soulseek waits**: when Encore's own schedule holds the reconnect, *Download now* connects now (never within 30 seconds of the last attempt, and at most every 2 minutes from presses). When the Soulseek server asked Encore to wait (it isn't answering logins, or another client logged in with this username), it says when the next attempt is and changes nothing, so the account isn't shut out. When Soulseek is stopped, the request goes to its next source.
- **A Soulseek download queued at the other user**: the row reads "Queued by alice – 12th in their queue, 0/12 files". *Download now* picks a user with a free upload slot from the answers kept from the request's own search, by the same rules as the automatic choice (format, bit rate, track count, unwanted versions, users already tried or blocked), queues the files there first and only then cancels them at alice. Not for a download you picked by hand or asked for with *Download anyway*: those asked for exactly those files.
- **A torrent or Usenet download with no progress for five minutes** (added at least two minutes ago, not waiting in its client's queue, not busy checking or unpacking its files): *Download now* removes it from the client, with its data, and the request goes to its next source. Not for a release you picked by hand.
- **No button** for a request that waits for approval, is searching, downloading with progress or importing; one whose files wait to be imported has *Import again*, and one that waits for its plugin to finish installing after the upgrade starts as soon as the plugin is there.
- **Download all now** (admins, on the Requests page beside *Retry failed*, following *Only mine*): presses every request in line in the order it stands, checks the storage once and tries Soulseek once. It never switches users, drops downloads or starts more than a source allows ("Nothing is waiting – every request is downloading or done" when there is nothing to do).
- Every press is one line in the activity log ("carol asked to download request #12 (Duumu – Illuminate EP) now: next in line for Deezer"). No database change.

#### Download folders that can't be written
- **What is checked**: every folder a download is saved in: the staging folder (yt-dlp, streamrip, links and plugins you added), Soulseek's downloads folder, the torrent and Usenet clients' folders as Encore sees them, and the watch folders. Every minute while downloads run, Encore writes a small file into each, flushes it to the disk and removes it. While nothing downloads, the folders are only looked at every 10 minutes, without writing, so idle disks can spin down; a source that starts after a quiet stretch gets a fresh check first, and *Check again* checks at once. Encore's own folders are made when they are missing; a client's folder is not, since an empty folder made in the container would hide a missing volume.
- **What it tells apart**: read-only, not writable by Encore's user, missing, full, and other disk errors. A network mount that stops answering counts as failing after 30 seconds ("… can't be written (it doesn't answer)").
- **Requests wait instead of failing**: a request whose source saves into a failing folder shows *Waiting for the downloads folder* ("Waiting for the downloads folder – /downloads/encore is read-only" for admins; members see "a storage problem on the server; the download starts when it is fixed"). No attempt is counted, no other source is tried and the indexers are not asked. It goes on by itself within seconds of the folder being fine again. A torrent or Usenet request waits only when no download client can save; a release for the client whose folder fails is skipped.
- **The banner** (admins, every page): "The downloads folder /downloads is read-only – check the disk (dmesg, fsck) or the mount", "… is missing – check the mount", "… is full – free some space on its disk", or "… isn't writable by Encore (uid 1000, gid 1000) – chown 1000:1000 … on the host, or set PUID/PGID to its owner" (for a folder Encore owns but may not write in: "… isn't writable: Encore (uid 1000) owns it, but it has no write permission – chmod u+w … on the host"), with *Check again*. Folders on one read-only or full disk are named by their parent.
- **What holds nothing up**: a download client's own folder that Encore can only read (mounted read-only in Encore's container, or owned by the client's user), and a SABnzbd or NZBGet folder that isn't in Encore's container. Diagnostics shows them with a note.
- **Diagnostics → Download folders** (website, desktop app, phone) lists each folder with its state, when it was checked and since when it fails; *Copy report* includes them. The activity log and `docker logs` get one line when a folder starts or stops holding up downloads ("downloads folder: /downloads/encore is read-only – requests that save there wait"). No database change.

#### Translated titles without the local model
Translated and romanized titles now come from several sources, strongest first; every one after the first can be switched off:

1. **Corrections by hand** (⋯ → *Edit translation*): always first, never replaced.
2. **Official names**: MusicBrainz pseudo-releases and aliases, Apple's English names for Japanese releases, and now an artist's sort name in Latin letters as its reading ("Yonezu, Kenshi" gives "Yonezu Kenshi").
3. **Romanization built into the server**, with no model and no network: Japanese kana in Hepburn (long vowels with macrons: おもう is "Omō"), Korean in Revised Romanization ("Eotteoke Ibyeolkkaji Saranghagesseo"), Cyrillic, Greek, and Hebrew, Arabic, Thai and Devanagari letter by letter (those only fill a gap: any other source replaces them). Titles with kanji and Chinese titles are left to the other sources. Korean is written syllable by syllable without the sound changes between syllables ("Dokrip", where the official spelling is "Dongnip").
4. **DeepL**, with your API key: song and album titles only, never lyrics or artist names, 50 at a time. Encore counts the characters it sends per month and stops at *DeepL characters per month* (400,000 by default, 0 for no limit; DeepL's free plan has 500,000). A refused key pauses DeepL for a day or until the key changes, DeepL's own quota for 6 hours, and a request that could not connect counts no characters.
5. **The local model** (the Translator plugin), only when it is installed: what the others leave. It needs several GB of memory and is not installed by default.

A stronger source replaces the text of a weaker one, and each title keeps where it came from (the website's edit dialog names it). **Settings → Server → Library → Translated titles** has the switches, the DeepL key and the budget. **Settings → Language** says in one line what this server uses, and admins get a *Translation sources* card with each source's state ("Romanization: built in", "DeepL: 12 340 of 400 000 characters this month", "Local model: not installed – install it in Plugins") and a switch per source, on the website, in the desktop app and on the phone. The database moves to schema 29 for this.

#### Files
- **Files** (admins only): Settings → System → Files on the website and in the desktop app; on the phone, Settings → Server → Files or *Files* in Home's ⋯ menu. Members never see it, and every request behind it checks that the caller is an admin.
  - **The folders it can reach:** the library (`library_path`), the staging folder, Soulseek's download folder, the torrent and Usenet download and watch folders that are there on the disk, and Trash (`/config/trash`). Each shows its free space; a folder Encore can't write to says *Read-only*. Nothing outside these folders can be reached: `../`, absolute paths and look-alike characters are refused with "That path is outside the folders Encore may use", and a link that points outside is listed but never followed.
  - **Browse:** a tree of the folders, breadcrumbs, a path box, sorting by name, size or date (folders first), 500 entries at a time. Songs the library knows show their artist, title and length. Picking works like a desktop file manager (click, Ctrl/⌘-click, Shift-click, Ctrl+A), and so do the keys: Enter opens, Backspace goes up, Delete moves to trash after asking, F2 renames. Every row has one ⋯ menu, which a right-click also opens.
  - **Upload** files or a whole folder (its subfolders kept). On the website you can also drag them onto the page; the desktop app takes files dropped on its window (Windows, macOS and X11; on Wayland use *Upload*). When a name is taken: *Keep both* ("Song (2).flac", the default), *Replace* (the old file goes to Trash) or *Skip*. A progress panel shows the file being sent and has *Stop*; a cut-off upload leaves nothing behind.
  - **New folder, Rename, Move** (a folder picker over the folders that can be written). Songs moved or renamed inside the library keep their place in the library: likes, playlist entries and plays follow them.
  - **Move to trash, Restore, Delete forever, Empty trash.** Trash is the only place files are deleted for good. Restore puts a file back where it was and brings its songs back with their likes and playlist entries. Items stay in Trash until you delete them.
  - **Edit tags** of any audio file (title, artist, album, album artist, track, disc, year, genre); an emptied field removes the tag. For songs the library has, *Edit song details…* opens the usual Fix song dialog.
  - **Import…** on a folder: every album in it, matched like the match review (*Other matches* searches MusicBrainz, Deezer, Apple Music, Discogs or by audio), then tagged and moved into the library. Nothing in the library is replaced (a taken name gets " (2)"), songs that were in the library already keep their likes, plays and shares, and nothing is deleted for good: what is left in an emptied folder (covers, .nfo, .cue, logs) and the library's lower-quality copies and loose singles it replaces go to Trash. With **Keep the files where they are** on, the songs join the library where and as they are: nothing is moved, renamed or retagged, and the folder is added to *Extra library folders* (not for Staging, which Encore clears, nor for a folder that holds the library).
  - Every change is a line in the activity log (who, what, from where to where).
- **Extra library folders** (Settings → Server → Library → *Library & tagging*, one folder per line; env `ENCORE_LIBRARY_EXTRA_ROOTS`). Encore scans them with the library folder (at start, on schedule and with the Library page's *Scan*), and never moves, renames or retags their files on its own: *Organize files*, renaming after *Edit details* and a release picked in the match review leave the files where they are, and the automatic re-tag of albums made from Deezer or Apple data, *Auto-tag untagged* and *Find missing artwork* skip them. Your own edits still write tags, and *Find missing artwork* keeps a cover it finds for them in Encore's own cover store instead. An import's duplicate check never deletes a song there: both copies stay. A folder that isn't there at scan time, or is empty while the library has songs in it (a disk not mounted leaves an empty folder), is skipped and its songs stay in the library; the scan's line names it, and the activity log says it once.

#### Before you update (the 0.33 part)
- **The database moves to schema 29** (the translation sources: two columns on the translations and a table for the DeepL count), after the backup described above. From 0.30 the steps to 27 and 28 run first.
- **Docker:** the file manager sees the server's folders as the container sees them. A folder you want to import and keep where it is has to be mounted into the container first; *Extra library folders* takes paths inside the container.
- **Trash lives in `/config/trash`.** When `/config` is on another disk than your music, *Move to trash* copies the files there (after checking there is room) and removes the originals, so trashing a large album takes a while and needs space on the config disk.
- **The first translation pass** after the upgrade replaces the local model's romanizations of kana, Korean, Cyrillic and Greek titles with the built-in ones, and with a DeepL key its translations of song and album titles (see *Before you update* at the top).

#### What to test (the 0.33 part)
- **Upload a folder** of two FLACs into Staging, then *Import…* it into the library with *Keep the files where they are* off; check the album in the library.
- **Move after a like:** like one of its songs, move the album to another folder of the library, and play the liked song from Liked Songs.
- **Rename, Move to trash and Restore** a file and a folder; then *Delete forever* and *Empty trash*.
- **Keep in place:** *Import…* a folder outside the library with *Keep the files where they are* on; check the songs are in the library, the files have not moved or changed, and the folder is listed under *Extra library folders*. Add a song to that folder by hand and press *Scan* on the Library page.
- **Refusals:** type `../` or `/etc` in the path box; sign in as a member and check Files is not there.
- **The phone and the desktop app:** the same steps from Settings → Files, an upload through the system picker on the phone, and a file dropped on the desktop app's window.
- **Uploads that stop:** close the tab or press *Stop* during a large upload, and check no hidden `.encore-upload` file is left in the folder (a torrent client's own `.part` files next to it are never touched).
- **Share a playlist and Liked Songs**: open the link in a private window and on a phone that is not signed in (*Play*, *Shuffle*, a song from the list), paste it into a chat app (the preview), remove a song and reload the page, then *Stop sharing* and reload (the link no longer works).
- **Add someone's playlist**: as a second account, *Add a shared playlist* with the link; the owner adds and removes a song and the second account sees it after a reload; *Save a copy*; the owner's page says "added by 1 person"; the owner stops sharing and the second account sees "… stopped sharing this playlist" with *Remove*. On the phone, share the link into Encore from a chat app. In the car, the added playlist is under Playlists.
- **Download now in a line**: request two Deezer albums; the second reads "Next in line for Deezer" (or "2nd …"). Press *Download now* on a later one and check it goes next and that only one streamrip download runs at a time. As admin, *Download all now* with a few requests waiting.
- **Download now on a stuck torrent**: a torrent with no seeds that has made no progress for five minutes (on a request with another source left to try) offers *Download now*; pressing it removes the torrent from the client and the request tries its next source.
- **The car, the error messages on the phone, the downloads mount and DeepL**: see *What to test on your hardware* at the top.

#### For API users and developers
- Playlists: `POST` and `DELETE /api/playlists/{id}/share` (the owner shares or stops sharing; `{token, path, url}`); `GET /api/playlists` and `/api/playlists/{id}` carry `share` (with `added_by` for the owner), `read_only` and `linked` (`{owner, active, liked}` for an added playlist, `kind: "linked"`). `GET /api/share/{token}` answers for playlists too (`offset`, `limit` up to 500, `shuffle=<seed>`, `viewer`); `POST /api/share/{token}/copy` saves a copy; `POST /api/playlists/shared` (`{link}`), `POST /api/playlists/shared/check` and `POST /api/share/{token}/add` add a shared playlist; `POST /api/playlists/{id}/copy` copies an added one. Every changing route answers 403 for an added playlist.
- Requests: `POST /api/requests/{id}/download-now` (the owner or an admin; 403, 404 or 409 with the reason when there is nothing to do) and `POST /api/requests/download-now` (admins; `{mine}`). `GET /api/requests` rows gain `can_download_now`, `download_now {act, title, until}`, `line`, `remote_queue`, `stalled_since` and `pressed`; a request in a line has `status: "queued"` and the line's words as its `message`. A wait for a downloads folder is `status: "waiting"` with `wait_reason: "waiting_disk"`.
- `GET /api/requests/storage` has a `downloads` part (admins: the folders, the banner's text and the times; members: `ok`), `GET /api/system/diagnostics` has `download_folders`, and `GET /api/translate/status` has `summary` and, for admins, `tiers`.
- Settings: `translate_official`, `translate_romanize`, `translate_deepl`, `deepl_api_key` (secret, `DEEPL_API_KEY`), `deepl_char_budget` and `translate_local_model`, each with an environment variable of the same name in capitals.
