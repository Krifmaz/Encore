## Encore 0.42.15

**Big playlists open at once, and missing songs get requested.** A playlist of thousands of songs (a big Liked
Songs) no longer shows a blank page on the web while it loads, and songs that were skipped by *Download missing*
because they had no artist are now requested or clearly listed.

### Details
- Web app: a long playlist shows "Loading playlist…", then its first songs straight away; the rest fill in
  while you scroll. Sorting a long playlist is much faster too.
- *Download missing* now reads the artist out of a song's own text when its artist is empty ("Artist - Song" in
  the title, or the video title it was imported from), so most of those songs get requested.
- Songs that still can't be requested (no artist, no MusicBrainz id) are marked *can't request*. A
  *N can't be requested* button (web, desktop) or badge (Android) lists them to copy, so you can fix or re-add them.
- Android: the hearts on Liked Songs now match the list. A liked song that arrived in the library since the app
  last checked your likes used to show an empty heart there.

**A Downloads tab in the Android app.** The phone app's bottom bar now has *Downloads* next to Playlists: the
Requests page, with everything the web's Requests page has, one tap away instead of behind the Home avatar menu.

### Downloads tab details
- The same buttons as the web: the *remaining* / *awaiting approval* / *failed* badges under the title, the admin's
  *User* list, *Retry failed*, *Download all now*, *Clear finished* and *Clear waiting*, then the *All / Active /
  Awaiting approval / Available / Failed* tabs with their counts.
- Each request keeps its queue position ("61st in line for Apple Music") and its *Pick*, *Download now* and cancel
  buttons, with the rest under ⋯.
- Home's *Requests* menu entry and *All requests* open the tab. On a TV, *Downloads* is in the side menu after
  Playlists.
