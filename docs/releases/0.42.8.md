## Encore 0.42.8

**A shared song opens the player.** Opening a song's share link used to land on its album page. Now it goes
straight to Now playing with the song playing:

- **Android app:** the song starts (the rest of its album queued after it, as if you'd tapped it on the album
  page) and the full player opens. On a TV the TV player opens.
- **Web player (signed in):** the same: the song plays from its album and the full-screen player opens. Browsers
  that block sound until you tap leave it paused in the open player, one tap on play away.
- Album, artist and playlist links still open their pages, as in 0.42.5.

### Details
- When the album can't be loaded, or no longer has the song, the song plays on its own; when the song left the
  library, the link goes to the browser as before.
- In the web player the `?track=` leaves the address once the song starts, so going back to the album (Back, a
  reload) doesn't start it again.
- Update the Android app to get this; older apps keep opening the album page.
