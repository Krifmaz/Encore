## Encore 0.42.12

**Recommendations count how much of a song you heard, and what you share.** Smart shuffle, the For you mixes and
"More like this" now learn from two more things you do.

- **Listen time:** a song you hear to the end counts for more than one you leave soon after it counted as a play
  (1.2 plays against 0.8). Older plays, and plays from apps that don't say yet, count as one play, as before.
- **Skips:** Next in the first seconds is still a full "not now"; Next just before the halfway mark counts only a
  fifth of that, and no longer drops a song out of your shuffles after a few times.
- **Shares:** a song you made a share link for counts a little more than a like, fading slowly over months. An
  album link counts like one shared song spread over the album. Playlist links don't count (the playlist already
  does).

### Details
- The weights are fixed, like the existing ones (no settings): a play 0.8 to 1.2 by how much was heard, a share
  5.0 with a 180-day half-life, a skip −0.6 down to −0.12 by how far in it came. *How Encore recommends music*
  (docs/recommendations) lists them all.
- The web app and the Android app tell the server how far a counted song got (`POST /api/taste/listened`). The
  desktop app counts plays as before.
- Database schema 42 adds `plays.listened`; the server backs up first, as with every schema change.
