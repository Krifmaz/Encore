## Encore 2026.10.11.1 – Encore 2 (Rust) is here

The first dated release. Encore is now a single Rust program with the web app built in. The Python server is gone, and so is the need for Lidarr. Keep your `/config` folder: the database, users, playlists and settings carry over.

### Highlights
- **Rust server**: faster, smaller, one binary. Docker images for amd64 and arm64.
- **Torrents & Usenet without Lidarr**: Prowlarr searches your indexers; releases go to qBittorrent or SABnzbd and are imported automatically.
- **Remote control and link mode**: control any of your signed-in devices, move music between them, or *link* a phone as a locked remote for your computer. *Unlink* to listen on the phone again.
- **Last.fm**: sign in with Last.fm to scrobble what you play (ListenBrainz scrobbling too). *For you* adds *Artists for you* and *Songs for you* from your Last.fm listening.
- **Appearance**: Dark, OLED black, Light or Match device; presets; any accent colour; the logo follows your colour. App icons are charcoal and eggshell.
- **Account**: change your username, profile picture, two-step sign-in, sign out everywhere.

### Settings
- API keys are back in each service's own card. The eye shows a saved key, including ones set in your compose file. A copy button sits next to it.
- The Encore API key moved to the bottom of General.
- *Test cookies* for YouTube Music; the Spotify test explains a missing ID or secret.
- Fixed the settings menu hiding behind the player and the account page running off-screen.

### Removed
- Following artists (playlist following stays).
- Lidarr settings: if you used Lidarr, set up Prowlarr instead (see the README).

### Upgrading from Encore 1
Switch the image to `ghcr.io/krifmaz/encore:latest` (or this version's tag). Remove the `lidarr` service and `LIDARR_*` variables from your compose file.
