## Encore 0.26.8

- **Right-click for actions.** A right-click (a long press on a phone) on a request, a song row, the album header or the player bar opens the same menu as that row's ⋯ button, at the pointer. Text fields keep the browser's own menu, and where there is no ⋯ nothing changes.
- **Album page rows show a play button** when the pointer is over them (in the number column), and songs without a track number in their tags show their place in the album instead of a dash.
- Server image only; the desktop and Android apps are the 0.26.0 builds. Reload the web app once after updating.
