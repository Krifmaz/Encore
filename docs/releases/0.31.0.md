## Encore 0.31.0

Volume normalization, a lyrics mode, more on the artist page, one search for the whole library, the car's Artist button and quality badge, and group listens: several people on this server hearing the same queue at the same time.

0.31 was not released on its own: it ships in **0.33.0** together with 0.32 (plugins) and 0.33 (the file manager and the work since). The combined notes, with what to do before you update from 0.30, are in [0.33.0.md](0.33.0.md). Where 0.33 changed something described here, the line says so.

### New
- **Volume normalization.** Songs play at the same loudness. The server reads each song's ReplayGain and R128 tags when it scans, and measures the songs without them in a new scheduled task, **Measure loudness** (Settings → Scheduled tasks): on by default, every 24 hours, at the lowest priority and only while nobody uses the app; each run goes on where the last one stopped.
  - **Website:** Settings → Playback → Sound (on this device): *Volume normalization* Off / Track / Album (Off by default), *Target level* (−23 to −5 LUFS, −14 by default) and *Prevent clipping* (on by default). The Signal path shows a *Normalization* row ("−3.2 dB (album gain, target −14 LUFS)").
  - **Phone:** the same three settings and the same Signal path line. The car plays through the phone, so it follows them.
  - **Desktop app:** the same three settings in Settings → Playback; the gain is applied only while *Play through Cadenza on this device* is on (bit-perfect playback leaves the volume alone).
  - **Linked devices:** the website controlling another website or a phone sees and changes that device's settings through the remote, and a song sent to another device keeps its loudness. The desktop app keeps its own settings for now.
- **Lyrics mode** on the website, the desktop app and the phone: the Lyrics tab of the full player shrinks the cover to a small bar at the top and gives the lyrics the whole column, with the line being sung in the middle, past lines dimmed, and a tap on a line of synced lyrics seeking to it. *Back to the cover* returns; the mode is remembered.
- **Long song titles scroll in the player instead of being cut off.**
- **Artist pages** show *Liked from this artist* (the songs you liked, with Play all) and *In your library* (the artist's songs, the first 10 with *Show all*, and the albums), between Popular and the discography. Website, desktop app and phone.
- **One search for the whole library.** On the Library page a search shows artists, albums and songs at once, whatever tab is open, each with *Show all N*; the search stays in the address, so back and reload keep it. Initials work: "rhcp" finds Red Hot Chili Peppers, "crazy gb" finds Crazy by Gnarls Barkley. When nothing matches, *Search everywhere* opens Discover with the search.
- **Discover searches the library too:** Top results starts with *In your library* (songs, albums and artists), and the Songs, Albums and Artists tabs show what the library has above the catalogue.
- **Android Auto:** an *Artist* button on the now-playing screen opens the artist (Shuffle, Popular songs, Albums, Liked from this artist), also from the Encore Lyrics car app. Android Auto shows the quality of the song (Lossless, Hi-res, Lossy or Cadenza, with "FLAC 24/96" and the like on the line under the title). **Changed in 0.33:** on the owner's car these two extra buttons pushed the row to three buttons and ⋯, and pressing them did nothing, because Android Auto lets a music app neither open its own pages from a button nor resize its player. In 0.33.0 the row is Shuffle, Repeat, Like and Lyrics, the quality is always on the line under the title, the artist is *More by <artist>* at the top of Browse, and Artist and Signal path are buttons you can add under *Car buttons* (see the 0.33 notes).
- **Group listen** (below).
- **Sign-ins started in the phone or desktop app** (Spotify, Tidal, Last.fm, MusicBrainz) work without signing in to Encore in the browser. A browser that is not signed in as you asks first: "Link the Spotify account … to the Encore user …?". That question can't protect a Last.fm sign-in (only Last.fm's own approval page does), so the Last.fm dialog adds "Only approve Encore on Last.fm when you started the sign-in yourself."
- **Settings set by the environment are locked** on the Users and Invites pages too (website and desktop app), with the same "🔒 Set by ENCORE_… in your compose/env" note as the Server pages.

### Fixes
- **Sample rates are written exactly** on the website, the desktop app and the phone (Signal path, the car's quality line, downloaded songs): 22050 Hz is "22.05 kHz" and 11025 Hz "11.025 kHz" (0.30 wrote "22.1 kHz"); 44.1 and 48 stay as they were.
- **The desktop app's Home tile** "things you requested · #1 on the leaderboard" no longer puts "leaderboard" outside the card: the rank has its own line.

### Group listen
Several people on this server listen to the same queue at the same time, each on their own device and with their own quality settings.

- **Starting and joining:** *Start a group listen* in the player's ⋯ menu or in Your devices (website, desktop app, phone). Others join with the code, the invite link (`<server>/#/group/<code>`; the phone takes it through Share or a paste), or from the list of live group listens: Home's *Listening together now* row and the *Group listens* section of Your devices. The host chooses who can see it: *Anyone on this server can see and join* (the default) or *Only people with the link*.
- **While in one:** the player shows *Group · 3*, which opens the group (the code, *Copy invite link*, the members with their devices, *Everyone can control*, *End for everyone* or *Leave*). The host controls playback unless *Everyone can control* is on; everyone with an account can add songs to Up next, which shows who added each one. A pause on your own device pauses only you (*Paused here · Catch up*). Crossfade is off inside a group (gapless stays). When the host leaves, their account keeps the group on another device it is in, else the person who has been in it longest takes over; the last one leaving ends it.
- **Keeping in step:** every device follows the server's clock. The website and the phone close small gaps by playing a touch faster or slower and seek past half a second; the desktop app keeps in step by seeking only (Cadenza has no speed control).
- **Guests:** with the admin's switch and the host's *Guests can join with the link* both on, people without an account can listen on a web page from the invite link, under a name of their choice. They can't add songs, control playback or browse the library, and nothing they hear is logged or scrobbled. Their access ends with the group, when they leave or are removed, when an admin turns guests off, or after 12 hours.
- **Admin settings** in Settings → Server → General → Group listens: *Allow group listens* (on), *Let people without an account join with the invite link* (off), *Most people in one group listen* (20, guests count; a host can set a lower limit) and *Keep group listens through a server restart* (on: groups come back paused where they were, the apps join again by themselves and the host presses play; off: a restart ends them). Group listens nobody used for 12 hours end.
- **Limits:** 10 group listens started per account an hour; wrong codes are limited per account, per address and for everyone without an account together.

### Before you update
- **The database moves to schema 27 on the first start**, after Encore saves a copy of it in the `backups` folder of `/config` (Settings → Diagnostics → Backups). To go back to 0.30, put that copy back as the database before starting the older image.
- **Behind a reverse proxy with a public address** (Cloudflare without a tunnel, a proxy on another server): Encore now reads a visitor's address from `X-Forwarded-For` only on connections from a trusted proxy, which by default means loopback and the private networks (a proxy in another container or on your LAN), and takes the address that proxy added, not what the visitor wrote. Set `ENCORE_TRUSTED_PROXIES` to your proxy's addresses or networks (comma-separated, with the private ones it still needs); otherwise every visitor counts as the proxy for sign-in limits and the like.
- **Measure loudness** reads every song's tags once and then measures the songs without tags, a few hundred per run while the app is quiet; on a large library the first runs take a few nights. Settings → Scheduled tasks shows how many are left.

### What to test
- **Group listen with two people:** two browsers signed in as two users (make the second user in Settings → Users). Start on one, join by code on the other; play, pause and seek on the host and check both play within about half a second; add a song from the member and see "added by" on both; pause on one device only and *Catch up*; let the host leave and check the other becomes the host.
- **The phone joining by link:** share the invite link to the app, join, and listen beside a browser.
- **Drift after a pause:** pause one device for a minute, *Catch up*, and check it lands where the group is.
- **Guests:** turn on the admin switch and the host's switch, open the invite link in a private window, pick a name, listen; then remove the guest, or turn the admin switch off and check the guest page ends.
- **A restart** with *Keep group listens through a server restart* on: the group comes back paused and the devices rejoin.
- **Normalization:** a song with ReplayGain tags and one without (after Measure loudness has run): the Signal path's Normalization row on the website, the phone and the desktop app.
- **Lyrics mode** at desktop and phone width; **the artist page** after liking two songs of an artist; **the library search** with "rhcp" and "crazy gb".
