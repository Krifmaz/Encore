## Encore 0.42.20

### New

* **Lantern under Settings → Connections** (admins). Save the address and API key of your Lantern server,
  test the connection, and preview which torrent, Usenet and Soulseek settings would move into it. Encore
  starts searching and downloading through Lantern once Lantern 1.0 is out: until then the card says so and
  nothing about your downloads changes. Servers without Lantern work exactly as before.
