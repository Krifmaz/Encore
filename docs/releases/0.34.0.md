## Encore 0.34.0

Two new download sources in the plugin catalog: **zotify** for Spotify and **gamdl** for Apple Music. Both are
Encore's own code, like yt-dlp and streamrip: a fresh install shows them under Settings → Plugins as available, not
installed, and Install puts the program into a venv of its own. Each has a picker tab and a place in the source order.

### New since 0.33.0
- **zotify (Spotify)**: searches through your own Spotify developer app and downloads with your Spotify account
  through [zotify](https://github.com/Googolplexed0/zotify) (Install puts in v0.21.0). Ogg Vorbis as Spotify
  streams it, up to 320k with Premium (160k free), or converted to MP3, AAC or Opus with ffmpeg. Songs and albums,
  ISRC first when a request has one, synced lyrics as .lrc.
  - **Signing in works without a browser on the server.** Press *Test* on the zotify card: it answers with a
    Spotify link. Open it, sign in, and paste the address your browser ends on
    (`http://127.0.0.1:4381/login?code=…`, a page that does not load) into *Sign-in link*, then *Test* again. The
    first round signs in your account, the second your developer app. Both stay signed in by themselves.
  - Search needs a developer app (developer.spotify.com → Dashboard → Create app, Web API, Redirect URI
    `http://127.0.0.1:4381/login`); its Client ID goes on the card. Since February 2026 such apps get 10 results per
    search and often no ISRC in them.
- **gamdl (Apple Music)**: searches the catalog of your storefront and downloads with your subscription through
  [gamdl](https://github.com/glomatico/gamdl) (Install puts in 3.9.1 from PyPI). AAC 256k on its own; ALAC (24-bit
  when Apple marks it hi-res) and Dolby Atmos through a wrapper-v2 server you run, falling back to AAC for songs
  without such a stream. The media-user-token from music.apple.com's cookies goes on the card (kept as a secret);
  with a wrapper it is optional.
- **Plugins**: a catalog plugin's `[python]` table can now name `overrides` (`uv pip install --override`), so a
  package that asks for a git URL installs without git in the image. zotify uses it for librespot, pinned to a fixed
  commit's archive.

### Before you update
- **Nothing changes until you install them.** Neither plugin is installed on an upgrade, and neither is in the
  default source order. To use them for requests, install the plugin, sign in on its card, then add `zotify` or
  `gamdl` to *Source order* (Settings → Downloads → Download pipeline), for example
  `tidal,deezer,gamdl,slskd,torznab,zotify,ytdlp`. Their picker tabs show *Install* or *sign in* until then.
- **Their terms.** Both download from streaming services with your own accounts, which those services' terms do not
  allow; Spotify can end a session or an account that fetches too fast. *Wait between tracks* on the zotify card
  (30 seconds avoids most "Failed fetching audio key" errors) and *Real-time factor* slow it down.
- **Without Docker**, both need uv and Python 3.14 on the host, as yt-dlp and streamrip do, and ffmpeg (gamdl always,
  zotify for anything but Ogg Vorbis).
- **Not checked against the live services before release**: the build machine could not reach Spotify or Apple
  Music. Search, sign-in and download parsing are covered by tests with recorded answers; try one song from each on
  your server first.
