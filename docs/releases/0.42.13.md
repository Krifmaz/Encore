## Encore 0.42.13

**Sort a playlist your way.** Every playlist, Liked Songs and playlists added from someone's link now have a Sort
menu: *Custom order* (the playlist's own), *Date added*, *Title*, *Artist*, *Album* and *Duration*, each ascending
or descending.

### Details
- Web app: the *Sort* select and the ↑/↓ button above the song list. Android: the Sort chip under Play / Shuffle,
  with *Reverse order* in its menu.
- Each playlist remembers its own sort on that device. *Date added* starts newest first and *Duration* longest
  first; the others start A to Z.
- Play, Shuffle, a tap on a song and ⋯ → Play next / Add to queue follow the order shown.
- Sorting only changes how the list is shown: the playlist's own order (and what other apps see) stays as it is,
  so *Custom order* always brings it back. Songs without an album or a known length go last.

**A Downloads tab in the Android app.** The phone app's bottom bar now has *Downloads* next to Playlists: the
Requests page, with everything the web's Requests page has, one tap away instead of behind the Home avatar menu.

### Downloads tab details
- The same buttons as the web: the *remaining* / *awaiting approval* / *failed* badges under the title, the admin's
  *User* list, *Retry failed*, *Download all now*, *Clear finished* and *Clear waiting*, then the *All / Active /
  Awaiting approval / Available / Failed* tabs with their counts.
- Each request keeps its queue position ("61st in line for Apple Music") and its *Pick*, *Download now* and cancel
  buttons, with the rest under ⋯.
- Home's *Requests* menu entry and *All requests* open the tab. On a TV, *Downloads* is in the side menu after
  Playlists.
