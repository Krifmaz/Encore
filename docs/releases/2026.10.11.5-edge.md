## Encore 2026.10.11.5-edge

- **Edit details** on every album page (admins): change the album name, album artist and song titles by hand. Files are renamed and moved to match (`Artist/Album/NN Title`), and the album is **locked** – automatic tagging, re-tagging and the nightly tag check leave it alone from then on. The ⋯ menu has the same entry, and **Edit song details** on a song locks its album too.
- **Wrong downloads are rejected instead of renamed**: when a source returns a different release (another artist, or songs that aren't on the requested album), Encore now says "Wrong release: the download is by X, not Y" and moves on to the next source. Before, it relabeled whatever it got with the requested album, artist and cover, so wrong music ended up in the library under the right-looking names.
- Imported files keep their own titles; positions in the track list are only used for files with no title at all. The requested album's cover is only embedded when the files really are that album.
