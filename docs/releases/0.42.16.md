## Encore 0.42.16

**Save a video from a link to your device.** No more sketchy "YouTube to MP4" sites: paste a video link
(YouTube, Vimeo and the ~1,800 other sites yt-dlp knows) in the Downloader and press *Save video to this device*.
The server downloads the video as an MP4, hands it to your browser, computer or phone, and deletes its copy. The
video never goes into your library and is never kept on the server.

### Video download details
- Web: the browser saves the MP4 like any download. Desktop app: you pick where it goes. Android: it lands in
  *Downloads* (the Downloads tab's link box has *Save video to this phone*).
- Up to 1080p, H.264 video with AAC audio first (plays on every phone and browser), merged into one MP4. One video
  per link, never a whole playlist; videos over 4 GB are refused.
- The server deletes the file the moment it was sent whole. A failed or cancelled download (✕) is deleted at once,
  one nobody fetched after 15 minutes, and anything left by a restart when the server starts.
- At most two videos download at once on the server, and three per account. Only the account that started a video
  can see or fetch it.
- Admins can turn it off per account: *Download videos from links to their own device* in the account's
  permissions (on by default). It needs the yt-dlp plugin.
