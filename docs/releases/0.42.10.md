## Encore 0.42.10

**Tap a download's cover to play it.** On the Requests (downloads) list, a tap on the cover of a finished request
plays it straight away – the song, or the whole album from its first song. A tap anywhere else on the row still
opens its details, as before.

### Details
- Works in the Android app, the web app and the desktop app. It plays the same thing as *Play* in the details.
- Requests whose music isn't in the library yet (queued, downloading, failed …) keep a plain cover.
- Android's *Saved on this phone* list does the same: a saved album's cover plays it; the rest of the row still
  opens the album page.
