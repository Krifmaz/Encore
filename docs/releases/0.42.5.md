## Encore 0.42.5

**Share links open where you already listen.** A link to a song, album, artist or playlist used to land everyone on
the public share page, even people with the app or already signed in. Now:

- **Android phones with the Encore app:** the link opens in the app. A song opens its album and starts playing,
  an album or artist opens its page, and your own playlist (or one you added) opens. Someone else's playlist asks
  "Add to my playlists?". This works in Chrome and the other Android browsers that hand links to apps.
- **Signed in to Encore in the browser** (any phone, iPhone included, or a computer): the link opens in the
  normal web player. A song opens its album with that song highlighted and scrolled into view; an album, artist or
  your own playlist opens its page.
- **Everyone else** gets the share page as before: no app, not signed in, and every chat app's link preview.

### Details
- When the app isn't installed, the browser falls straight back to the link with `?app=0`, which skips the app
  step. The app does the same when it can't open the link itself (no account on the phone, another server's link,
  a link that ended), so you never get bounced back and forth.
- Link previews (Discord, X, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack …), an app's built-in browser and X's player card always
  get the share page with its preview card, as before.
- Someone else's playlist you haven't added yet still opens the share page in the browser, where you can add it.
- New: `GET /api/share/{token}/open` (signed in) says where a link opens: `{kind: "track", track_id, album_id}`,
  `{kind: "album", album_id}`, `{kind: "artist", name}`, or `{kind: "playlist", own, added, playlist_id, name}`.
  The web app's album page takes `?track=<id>` to pick out a song.
- The Android app answers `encore://<server>/…/s/<token>` links. Update the app to get this; older apps simply
  leave the link in the browser.
