## Encore 0.36.0

**Clean up your library by type, see who requested what, and group listen that behaves.** If you skipped
0.35.2, its fixes are in here too (imports first in MusicBrainz's line, group listen without skips, Clear buttons):
see [0.35.2](https://github.com/Krifmaz/Encore/blob/ccr-a4585b2e-d7xdtf/docs/releases/0.35.2.md).

### Library
- **Delete albums by type.** Library → Albums has a **Type** filter: Studio albums, Live, Compilations, Remixes,
  Soundtracks, Demos, DJ mixes, Mixtapes, Karaoke/Instrumental, Singles & EPs, One-offs. Admins get **Select**:
  tap albums or "Select all N", then **Delete…** – it asks first ("Delete 143 albums (38 GB)?") and moves them to
  the **Trash**, where Restore brings them back. Deleting one album also goes to the Trash now.
- How the type is known: MusicBrainz's release type when Encore knows it, the file's RELEASETYPE tag, then the
  title ("Live at…", "(Live)", "Unplugged", "in Concert" …). A new scheduled task, **Sort albums by type**, looks up
  albums it isn't sure about in the background, a few hundred at a time.
- **One-offs.** A song whose only "album" in your library is a compilation, remix album, various-artists release
  or soundtrack you have one or two songs of now sits on a **One-offs** shelf on the artist's page instead of filling
  the Albums grid with 1-song albums.
- **Change type…** on an album page: Album, EP, Single, One-off or Automatic. Your choice wins and survives rescans.
- **Who requested it.** Album pages and the song ⋯ menu say "Requested by Caz on Oct 13", "Uploaded by Caz",
  "Imported by Caz" or "Found in the music folder". It is stored on the songs, so Clear finished no longer erases
  it. Albums whose request was cleared before this update say "Requested by someone – record cleared".

### Group listen
- **Play adds to the group's Up next** instead of replacing it (Shuffle adds shuffled): "Added 12 songs to the
  group's Up next". Play only starts the group when nothing is playing and Up next is empty.
- **A pause is only yours.** Switching tabs, another app, a call, unplugging headphones, the car or your own Pause
  button pause only you ("Paused for you – the group keeps playing"); Play jumps to where the group is now.
  **Pause for everyone** is in the player ⋯ menu and the group panel for people who control the group.

### Requests
- **Filter by user.** Admins get a **User** list on the Requests page ("All users", "Caz (612)" …); Retry failed,
  Download all now, Clear finished and Clear waiting act on the person picked.
- **Import again on a stuck import.** An import that hasn't moved for 15 minutes gets **Import again**, which stops
  the stuck import and starts it over with the downloaded files. Encore also restarts on its own any search or
  import whose work stopped.

### Before you update
- The database goes from schema 30 to 31 (who added each song, album types). Encore backs it up first; going back to
  0.35 means restoring that backup.
