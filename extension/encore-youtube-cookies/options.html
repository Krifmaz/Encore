Encore – YouTube sign-in

Connects this browser to your Encore server. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Hand over a YouTube sign-in Recommended

Encore gets a YouTube session of its own and keeps it alive by itself, also while this computer is off.

  1. A new private window opens at Google's sign-in.
  2. Sign in there (any account; a separate one for Encore is fine).
  3. When YouTube Music shows you signed in, the sign-in is sent to Encore after a 3-second countdown and the private window closes – without signing out, so no browser uses that session again.

Sync this browser's own sign-in

The older way: sends this browser's YouTube cookies when the browser starts, when the sign-in changes and every 6 hours. Encore never refreshes them (that would sign this browser out), so it only works while this browser is used. Encore prefers a handed-over sign-in when it has one.
