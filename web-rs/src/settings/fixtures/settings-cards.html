Which sources are searched for a request, and in what order. The first one that has it wins.

Release types

Which releases Request discography, the artist pages and New releases count, like the metadata profiles in Lidarr. Song requests skip other versions (live, remix, sped up and so on) unless the song asked for is one.
Kinds to request
Request discography gets these. Each request can change them in its own panel.Default · env var RELEASE_TYPES
Skip releases that are
A release with any ticked extra is left out. Instrumental, acoustic, sped up, karaoke and covers are read from the titles.Default · env var RELEASE_SKIP

Qobuz / Tidal / Deezer (streamrip)

Searches Qobuz, Tidal and Deezer with your own accounts and downloads lossless (FLAC). Usually the best quality. streamrip is only used for these three: its SoundCloud support would give the same quality as yt-dlp, so SoundCloud links go through yt-dlp.
Downloads lossless from your own paid accounts. Respect each service’s terms – use at your own risk.Default · env var STREAMRIP_ENABLED

Torrents & Usenet (Prowlarr)

Searches your torrent and Usenet indexers (through Prowlarr) for whole albums and sends them to your torrent client (qBittorrent, Transmission, Deluge or rTorrent) or Usenet client (SABnzbd or NZBGet), or drops them into a watch folder.
Albums only. Prowlarr searches every indexer you added there; torrents go to the torrent client below, Usenet to the Usenet client.Default · env var TORZNAB_ENABLED

Soulseek

Searches the Soulseek file-sharing network – great for FLAC and rare releases. Encore runs Soulseek for you: enter any username and password and an account is created at the first sign-in. Forward the port for the best speeds.
Default · env var SLSKD_ENABLED
The network runs on sharing; many users refuse downloads to people who share nothing. Only the library folder is shared, read-only.Default · env var SOULSEEK_SHARE
VPN for Soulseek
Only Soulseek goes through the VPN; Tidal, Qobuz, Deezer and everything else keep your normal connection. No gluetun container needed.Default · env var VPN_ENABLED
Default · env var VPN_KILL_SWITCH

YouTube Music & links (yt-dlp)

Last-resort source: finds the song on YouTube Music. yt-dlp also downloads SoundCloud links you paste in the Downloader, along with YouTube, Bandcamp, Mixcloud and about 1,800 other sites.
Searches YouTube Music for requests, and downloads links you paste in the Downloader from YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Mixcloud and ~1,800 other sites.Default · env var YTDLP_ENABLED
Default · env var ENCORE_YTDLP_LAST_RESORT

YouTube sign-in

Signs yt-dlp in to YouTube: YouTube Music Premium unlocks 256 kbps audio, and signed-in downloads see fewer bot checks. Best: connect the Encore browser extension and hand over a sign-in from a private window – Encore then owns that session and keeps it alive by itself. Pasted cookies are the backup.
Only for pasted cookies while they are in use: Encore renews them the way an open YouTube tab does and saves the new ones. A handed-over sign-in is always refreshed every 4 hours; an everyday browser’s cookies never are (that would sign that browser out).Default · env var YOUTUBE_LIGHT_REFRESH

Media servers

Roon

Encore as a Roon extension: your Roon zones show up under Your devices with what they play, transport and volume, and “Play in Roon” starts a search result in a zone. Enable it here, then approve Encore once in Roon → Settings → Extensions.
Roon Core (or Nucleus / ROCK) on the same network. Roon 1.8 or newer.Default · env var ROON_ENABLED

Server processing (Cadenza)

Lets permitted users have their streams upsampled on this server by Cadenza (long-filter upsampling, dither and noise shaping, lossless 24-bit FLAC out). Off by default: it costs real processor time per stream. Allow users under People → Users & permissions.
Default · env var CADENZA_SERVER_ENABLED

Email

Lets Encore send password-reset and sign-in emails.

Notifications

Keeps liked songs and playlists in step with Spotify, YouTube Music, Jellyfin, Plex and the rest. Runs as the “Sync likes & playlists” task above.
